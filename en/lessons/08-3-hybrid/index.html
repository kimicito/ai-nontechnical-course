8.3 Hybrid Tasks

⏱ 20 min · for procurement specialists
Hybrid approach
General structure and methodology in external AI.
Real data (prices, supplier names, volumes) in on-prem.

Principle: methodology outside, data inside

The more sensitive the data (prices, contract terms, negotiation positions) — the stricter the on-prem. General methodology (document structure, analysis categories) goes to external AI.

Anonymization rules for cloud:
  • Remove: real prices, supplier names, contract terms, volumes
  • Keep: product categories, general requirements, methodologies
  • Replace: "Supplier X" instead of real names
