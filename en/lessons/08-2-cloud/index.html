Level 8 — Procurement

8.2 External Tasks: Cloud

⏱ 15 min · for procurement specialists
Open data (external AI)
All tasks in this lesson use public sources, general methodologies, open standards. You can use Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini.

Why cloud is better for external procurement tasks

  • Web search: supplier market analysis, pricing benchmarks
  • Methodologies: developing general templates (RFQ, RFP, tender documents)
  • Benchmarking: comparing procurement practices across industries
  • Standards: ISO, INCOTERMS, trade terms
Important: before sending to cloud — check that the request contains no real prices, supplier names, or contract details. If it does — anonymize or use on-prem.

1. Market analysis and supplier search

Prompt example (Claude with web search)
Find pump suppliers for industrial applications:

Requirements:
- Flow rate: [SPECIFY] m³/h
- Head: [SPECIFY] m
- Medium: [SPECIFY]

Tasks:
1. Top 5 global manufacturers
2. Model range with specs and approximate prices
3. Authorized dealers in [SPECIFY REGION]
4. Lead times
5. Warranty terms

No need for specific supplier quotes — general market overview.

Result: market overview with sources. Saves 2–3 hours.

2. Developing RFQ/RFP templates

Prompt example
Prepare a template RFQ for industrial equipment procurement:

1. General provisions (scope, timeline)
2. Technical requirements [SPECIFY]
3. Commercial terms (price format, delivery, payment)
4. Qualification requirements for bidders
5. Evaluation criteria (price, technical compliance, delivery time)
6. Required documentation
7. Submission format and deadline

Placeholders for company-specific data: [SPECIFY].

3. Benchmarking procurement KPIs

Prompt example
Benchmark procurement KPIs for manufacturing industry (2024–2026):

Questions:
1. What procurement cost-to-revenue ratios do leaders have?
2. Average savings from competitive bidding (%)
3. Typical supplier consolidation ratios
4. Payment terms benchmarks (days)
5. Digital procurement adoption rates

Structured report with sources.

- Who pays for what (transport, insurance, customs)

Practical examples with cost allocation.
