Level 9 — Construction

9.2 External Tasks: Cloud

⏱ 15 min · for construction managers
Open data (external AI)
Tasks using public standards, methodologies, and market data. Use Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini.

1. Building codes and standards research

Prompt example
Explain Russian building codes (СП, СНиП) requirements for warehouse construction:

Focus on:
- Foundation requirements for [SPECIFY] soil type
- Fire safety categories
- Load-bearing capacity standards
- Energy efficiency requirements (Теплозащита)
- Accessibility requirements

Structured answer with code references (СП 70, СП 118, etc.).

2. Construction methodology research

Prompt example
Compare construction methods for [SPECIFY]:

Options:
1. Monolithic concrete
2. Prefabricated concrete
3. Steel frame
4. Mixed

Criteria:
- Cost per m² (approximate ranges)
- Construction speed
- Durability
- Maintenance requirements
- Suitability for [SPECIFY CLIMATE]

Decision matrix with recommendations.

3. Equipment and material market analysis

Prompt example
Research the market for [SPECIFY EQUIPMENT] for industrial construction:

1. Top 5 manufacturers globally and in Russia
2. Key specifications and price ranges
3. Lead times for delivery
4. Warranty terms
5. Service network coverage

General market overview, no specific pricing needed.
