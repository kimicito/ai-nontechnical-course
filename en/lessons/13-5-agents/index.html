Level 13 — MRO & Maintenance

13.5 Agent Scenarios for MRO

⏱ 10 min · predictive maintenance, automated scheduling, spare parts alerts
How is an agent different from a language model?
An LLM answers requests. An agent works continuously: monitors equipment, predicts failures, schedules maintenance — without the command "check now". More details — Level 4.

Scenario 1: Predictive Maintenance Agent

Task: monitor equipment sensors, predict failures, schedule maintenance before breakdown.

[Trigger: sensor data every 15 min]
↓
[SCADA / IoT sensors]
├─→ Vibration levels
├─→ Temperature
├─→ Pressure
├─→ Current/power consumption
└─→ Oil analysis data
↓
[On-prem LLM: prediction model]
├─→ Baseline deviation detection
├─→ Trend analysis (degradation rate)
├─→ Failure probability (next 7/30/90 days)
└─→ Recommended action
↓
[Alerts]
├─→ Green: normal
├─→ Yellow: schedule maintenance within 2 weeks
└─→ Red: critical, stop and inspect immediately
↓
[Auto-create work order in CMMS]
Result: 30-50% reduction in unplanned downtime. Maintenance before failure, not after.

Scenario 2: Spare Parts Reorder Agent

Task: monitor spare parts consumption, predict demand, auto-generate purchase orders.

[Cron: weekly]
↓
[Inventory system]
├─→ Current stock levels
├─→ Consumption rate (last 3/6/12 months)
├─→ Open purchase orders
└─→ Critical equipment list
↓
[On-prem LLM: demand forecasting]
├─→ Safety stock calculation
├─→ Reorder point check
├─→ Lead time buffer
└─→ Critical spares priority
↓
[Auto-actions]
├─→ Stock OK: log only
├─→ Below reorder point: generate PO draft
└─→ Critical spare low: urgent alert to procurement
↓
[Notification to maintenance manager + procurement]
← Catalog Catalog
