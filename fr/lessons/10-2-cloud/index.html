10.2 Tâches externes Audit — cloud

⏱ 15 min · pour auditeurs
Données ouvertes (IA externe)
Tâches utilisant des normes d'audit publiques, des méthodologies d'audit et des exigences réglementaires. Utilisez Claude, ChatGPT ou Gemini.

1. Recherche de normes et méthodologies d'audit

Exemple de prompt
Explain ISA (International Standards on Auditing) requirements for:

1. Risk assessment procedures (ISA 315)
2. Internal control evaluation (ISA 330)
3. Audit evidence requirements (ISA 500)
4. Going concern assessment (ISA 570)
5. Related party transactions (ISA 550)

Include practical application examples for manufacturing companies.

2. Recherche de conformité réglementaire

Exemple de prompt
Research Russian regulatory requirements for internal audit:

1. Federal Law 307-FZ requirements
2. Bank of Russia regulations (for financial institutions)
3. Corporate governance code provisions
4. Reporting requirements to audit committee
5. Whistleblower protection regulations

Structured summary with compliance checklist.

3. Benchmarking des pratiques d'audit

Exemple de prompt
Benchmark internal audit practices for manufacturing industry:

1. Audit plan coverage (% of processes audited annually)
2. Staffing ratios (auditors per 1000 employees)
3. Technology adoption (data analytics, continuous auditing)
4. Reporting lines (to CEO, Audit Committee, Board)
5. Key performance indicators for audit function

Include industry averages and best practices.
